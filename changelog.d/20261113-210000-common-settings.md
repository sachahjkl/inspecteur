type: Changed

**The settings every program of gui_toolkit has**: the settings now have pages for the text and its fonts, scrolling, input, delays and menus, rendering and where messages show, beside the look (density, rounding, animations, glow) and Inspecteur's own; a technical setting is under a folded Advanced part, found by the search. The look's settings are renamed (`view.theme` is `appearance.theme`, `view.mode` `appearance.mode`, `view.finish` `appearance.finish`, `view.font_size` `appearance.font_size`, `view.zoom` `appearance.zoom`, `view.system_title` `window.system_title`): a theme, text size or zoom chosen before is not read again. The text size goes from 10 to 24 pixels. The tables' stripes and lines have a Tables page.
